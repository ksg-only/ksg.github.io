In August 2019, I arrived in the United States to study at Arizona State University. Basketball courts used to my outlet when I first arrived here, to socialize with other folks around the neighborhood.

Then March 2020 - Covid hits and university asked us to study remotely. Almost within two weeks, no courts, no friends, no games. I was stuck at home in a country I'd barely begun to explore, with nothing but classes and assignments all day. 

That's when I found this bike - an old Schwinn Trailway, a women's aluminum frame that my sister used to ride back in her school days at ASU and was comically small for my 6-foot frame. It wasn't the pretties bike, but it rolled when you pedal and I guess that was enough.

What started as a simple morning workout became something bigger. Each ride pushed me a little further my house, a little deeper into the city (Chandler, AZ) I barely knew. Streets I'd never walked down, desert sunrises I would've slept through otherwise - all of it opened up one pedal stroke at a time.
Chandler is a city on the very south of Phoenix area, and every morning I used to find a new southmost spot and clicked my bike from different vantage points around the city.

I didn't know it then, but that undersized Schwinn was the beginning of something that would reshape how I move through the world.
